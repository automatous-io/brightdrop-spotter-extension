How a BrightDrop VIN is read
2G5ZJ3TZ3S9100388
2G5BrightDrop through 2025. From 2026 it is 2GC, Chevrolet's truck code. All built at CAMI in Ingersoll, Ontario. Pos 6Model. 2 is a Zevo 400, 3 is a Zevo 600. Pos 8Powertrain. 6 FWD Standard, Y AWD Standard, Z AWD Max Range. Pos 9Check digit. Anything that fails it is never treated as a VIN. Pos 10Model year. S is 2025.
Where the data comes from
NHTSAThe U.S. build record GM files for every van. Supplies series, drive, motor and battery codes. ETJGM's Max Range battery code. 20 modules, 173 kWh. EWUExtended Range, new for 2026. 121 kWh. ETCGM's Standard Range battery code. 12 modules, 102 kWh. VINThe positions above are an offline cross-check. If they disagree, NHTSA wins. StickerGM's original build sheet for that exact van. On request, the card reads it for outlets, charger, price, color and first dealer.
Privacy and fine print
SentVINs go to NHTSA. A VIN goes to GM only when you request its sticker. StoredResults cached on this device for 30 days. TrackedNothing. No analytics, accounts or telemetry. RangeGM-estimated for the van's year and drive. Real range varies. AffiliationNone. Independent of GM and BrightDrop. Verify against the window sticker.
