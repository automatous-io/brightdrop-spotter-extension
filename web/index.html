Good to know

The three batteries
Max RangeGreen is the biggest battery. 173 kWh, up to 285 miles. All-wheel drive only.
Extended RangeAmber is the middle one. 121 kWh, up to 204 miles. New for 2026.
Standard RangeGrey is the smallest. 102 kWh, 159 to 179 miles depending on the year.
Where the VIN says it
2G5ZJ3TZ3S9100388
6thSize. 2 is a 400, 3 is the longer 600. 8thBattery and drive. Z is Max Range with all-wheel drive. Y is Standard with all-wheel drive. 6 is Standard with front-wheel drive. 10thModel year. R is 2024, S is 2025, T is 2026.
Where the answers come from
BatteryFrom NHTSA, the U.S. government's vehicle database, where GM files how every van was built. RangeGM's estimate for that battery, model year and drive. Real range changes with load, speed and weather. StickerStraight from GM. It is the original window sticker for that exact van, and it is only fetched when you ask for it.
Privacy
SharedThe VIN goes to the government database to look it up, and to GM only if you ask for the sticker. SavedYour recent lookups are kept in this browser and nowhere else. TrackedNothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Official?No. This is an independent project, not connected to GM or BrightDrop. Double-check anything important against the window sticker.

Shopping on a computer?

The Chrome extension does this for you as you browse. It tags every BrightDrop on dealer and listing sites with its battery, so there is nothing to paste.
